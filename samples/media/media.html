Photos and video no borders, no hover

Photos are plain and square. Video gets a square pause and play button in the bottom-right corner.

Photos

Photos have no border, no rounded corners and no hover effect. A caption sits underneath in the muted gray.

Video

This video starts on its own, muted and looping, like a hero video. The square button in the corner pauses and plays it. If your system is set to reduce motion, it starts paused instead. Tab to the button to see its focus ring, and hover it to see the accent color.

The pause button also matters for accessibility: anything that moves for more than five seconds should be stoppable.
